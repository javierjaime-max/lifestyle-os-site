Quick questions

How long does it take to go through?
The guide reads in 75–90 minutes the first time. Then you live in the action cards for 28 days — about 5–10 minutes a day to set up each day's experiment.
What if I'm a beginner / never worked out / haven't dieted?
Better. The actions are designed for the body you have today, not for someone already three years into training. The point is finding your one blocker — beginners often see it faster.
What if I've tried everything — calorie counting, fasting, peptides, all of it?
Then you've collected data on what hasn't worked, which is more useful than starting cold. The 28-Day Upgrade is the diagnostic that maps what hasn't worked to why. The "why" is what changes things.
Do I need a gym, equipment, special food?
No. Some actions involve a 20-minute strength session — bodyweight counts. Some involve protein at meals — eggs count. Nothing requires buying anything new.
Will I lose weight in 28 days?
Maybe a few pounds. Maybe not. The honest answer: most people don't see big composition shifts in a month, and anyone selling you 30-day transformation is lying. What 28 days gives you is the data on which actions work for your body. The bigger shift takes 90+ days using the actions you discovered.
